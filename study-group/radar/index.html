How the score works

A rough 0 to 100 number that ranks candidates by how much they are worth a look. It blends community interest, freshness and how close the paper is to our own work, each scaled from 0 to 1 and then weighted.

  • Hacker News points 30% Log-scaled, so the first points count most; it saturates around 400 points.
  • Hacker News comments 10% Also log-scaled, saturating around 250 comments.
  • Hugging Face upvotes 30% Log-scaled, saturating around 200 upvotes.
  • Recency 15% Halves every 14 days since publication. Older papers that come back on HN or Hugging Face get a flat middle value.
  • Relevance to our work 30% Strongest for recommenders and Next Best Action, then causal inference and uplift and applied finance (90%), less for bandits and tabular or time series (40%), scaled by how clearly the paper matches the topic.

The weights add up to 115%, so this is a relative score, not a percentage, and it can pass 100. If the optional triage step judges a paper hard to present in ten minutes, its score is multiplied by 0.85. It is a heuristic to rank candidates, not a judgement of quality.
