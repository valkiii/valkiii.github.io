Before you post

Contributions are public on GitHub and need a free GitHub account. Never include internal data, customer information or confidential material, and keep to published work.

Contributions arrive as discussions in valkiii/valkiii.github.io. The curator reads them with tools/sg_promote.py list and turns them into library entries, session updates and paper notes with add-paper, mark and add-notes. The workflow is described in content/study-group/README.md.
