PULP

Slice fruit with your hands. Or with your eyes.

Allow the camera when the browser asks. Point at the screen with your index finger and swipe through the fruit.

How to play

Hands — your index fingertips are blades. Swipe fast through fruit; slow moves don't cut. Both hands work. Don't slice bombs. Let 3 fruit fall in Classic and it's over. Zen has no bombs, 90 seconds.

Eyes (beta) — calibrate first (follow the dot). Then a fruit is cut by flicking your eyes across it or by staring at it for a moment. A bomb costs a life instead of the game. Close your eyes for a second to pause.
