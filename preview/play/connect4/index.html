Four in a row against five networks

Five convolutional networks learned Connect 4 by playing hundreds of thousands of games, then won a tournament against 35 other AIs as a team. They run in your browser. The washes under the board show how much the team likes each column before it moves.

How it decides

Each network sees the board as two 6 × 7 layers, its discs and yours, and scores all seven columns. The team averages the scores with fixed weights and plays the best legal column. Under the board you can see how many of the five would have chosen the same column on their own.
