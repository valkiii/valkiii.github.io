Team retro, facilitated

Can an AI facilitator keep a 60-minute retro on time?

A web app for running a team retrospective in person. Everyone joins by phone, Claude facilitates out loud, and a live screen turns notes into themes, votes and actions.

60minutes, nine steps
3votes each

The idea

Retros drift. A facilitator that watches the clock, groups the notes and asks the next question lets the team lead talk with everyone else.

How it works

  1. 1Write

    Silent notes on phones: keep, change, try, appreciate.

  2. 2Group

    Claude groups the notes into themes and presents them.

  3. 3Decide

    Three votes each; the top themes get root causes, options and owners with dates.

What I learned

  • Timeboxes enforced by a screen are easier to accept than ones enforced by a person.
