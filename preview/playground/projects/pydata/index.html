Hunting unicorns with networks

Can the network of who-worked-where predict the next unicorn?

The essay

My PyData Amsterdam 2024 talk. Start-ups linked by the people who moved between them, and what centrality says about their future.

The idea

A start-up's position in its ecosystem, measured through the people who move between companies, carries information about its future.

How it works

  1. 1Build

    A bipartite network of people and companies, projected onto companies.

  2. 2Measure

    Centrality over time as an early signal.

  3. 3Compare

    Against the track record of venture capital picks.
