I am a data science professional with more than eight years of experience in banking and fintech, in large and small organisations. I work in Python, big data and machine learning, and I like solving business problems with data, in a team, by trying ideas early.

Today I lead a data science team in retail banking in Amsterdam, working on churn, propensity to buy and recommender systems. Before that I was a data scientist at Barclays in London, and lead data scientist at FXCintel.

I received my PhD from the School of Mathematical Sciences at Queen Mary University of London, in the Complex Systems and Networks group of Prof. Vito Latora. Networks still shape how I look at almost everything.

Experience

Data Science Team Lead, ABN AMRO (2022 – today). Leads a team of 10 data scientists in the retail banking centre of excellence. Model-driven audience selection raised conversions by 30–70%; a recommender raised newsletter engagement by 80%.

Research consultant, NDA (2022). Historical study of founder-led, VC-backed tech IPOs against the market over 20 years, for the CEO of a fund.

Senior Data Scientist, ABN AMRO (2021 – 2022). Scientific lead of the team; NPS drivers, churn and propensity models, a collaborative-filtering recommender, member of the Data Science Review Board.

Consultant, Helium Foundation (2021). LondHeNet: performance indicators and placement optimisation for Helium hotspots in London.

Senior Data Scientist, Barclays (2019 – 2021). Forecasting UK spending from transactions during COVID, early-stage start-up discovery with Barclays Ventures and Eagle Labs, founder of the Data Bites talk series.

Lead Data Scientist, FXCintel (2017 – 2019). Dynamic FX price optimisation, lead generation from unstructured data, crawlers in production.
