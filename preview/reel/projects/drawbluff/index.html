Drawbluff

Can I host a Drawful-style party game from a laptop?

When
October 2026
Status
playable
Made with
Node.js (no dependencies), Server-Sent Events, Canvas, Kokoro TTS, WebAudio

Party games are best on a TV with everyone's phone as the controller. I wanted one that runs from my own laptop, without accounts, app stores or a subscription.

3–10players
1,251prompts
0npm packages needed to play

How it works

  1. 1
    Join. The TV shows a QR code. Phones scan it and join with a four-letter room code.
  2. 2
    Draw. Everyone draws themselves first, then a secret prompt. Bots fill empty seats.
  3. 3
    Bluff. For each drawing, players write a title others might mistake for the real one, then vote.
  4. 4
    Host. A local Kokoro voice presents the game, over a soundtrack generated in WebAudio.

What I learned

Server-Sent Events plus plain POST requests are enough for a real-time party game.
Wording matters. Players found "write a lie" off-putting; "write a title" felt like a game.
