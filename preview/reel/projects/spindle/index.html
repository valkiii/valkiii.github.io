Spindle

Can I make the Mac widget I wanted, for less?

When
September 2026
Status
in progress
Made with
Swift, SwiftUI, AppKit, AppleScript

Small Mac utilities are often good and slightly too expensive. Spindle is my take on the album-art widget, priced lower.

0API keys or logins
7day trial before a licence

How it works

  1. 1
    Listen. AppleScript asks Spotify or Music what's playing every second; no accounts involved.
  2. 2
    Show. A desktop tile with the cover, controls on hover, and a notch player that expands.
  3. 3
    Sell. A licence check with a trial, built to work offline.

What I learned

Swift swallows AppleScript syntax errors into vague runtime failures; test the script on its own first.
