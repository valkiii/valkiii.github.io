SQL Sprint

Can you prepare for a live SQL interview in a week?

When
October 2026
Status
shipped
Made with
sql.js (SQLite in WebAssembly), JavaScript

I had a live SQL coding interview coming. The best preparation is writing queries against data and being told exactly what's wrong.

49graded exercises
2timed mock interviews
32concept questions

How it works

  1. 1
    Learn. Six days from foundations to window functions and product metrics: retention, funnels, A/B readouts, churn.
  2. 2
    Check. 'Check answer' compares your output with the solution and says what's off: row count, order, rounding, integer division.
  3. 3
    Rehearse. Day 7 is two 45-minute mock interviews with the clarifying questions to ask.

What I learned

Feedback on why an answer is wrong teaches more than the right answer.
It now sells as an offline course.
