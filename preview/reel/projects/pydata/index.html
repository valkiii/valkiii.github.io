Hunting unicorns with networks

Can the network of who-worked-where predict the next unicorn?

When
September 2024
Status
talk
Made with

The essay →

A start-up's position in its ecosystem, measured through the people who move between companies, carries information about its future.

How it works

  1. 1
    Build. A bipartite network of people and companies, projected onto companies.
  2. 2
    Measure. Centrality over time as an early signal.
  3. 3
    Compare. Against the track record of venture capital picks.
