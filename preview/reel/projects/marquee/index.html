Can a Raspberry Pi be my Plex?

When
October 2026
Status
in use
Made with
Python, ffmpeg, HLS (fMP4), hls.js, Tailscale, Chromecast

Remote playback in Plex stopped working for me. A server that only does what I need, over my private network, looked smaller than debugging it.

0.1×realtime: why it never transcodes video
7×realtime audio conversion
0ports open to the internet

How it works

  1. 1
    Never transcode. The Pi decodes HEVC at a tenth of real time, so video is always copied as is and only audio is converted.
  2. 2
    Cut cleanly. Segments start on keyframes from a precomputed index, so seeking is instant.
  3. 3
    Reach. Tailscale makes it work the same at home and away, with HTTPS and nothing exposed.

What I learned

Knowing the hardware's limits up front shapes the whole design.
