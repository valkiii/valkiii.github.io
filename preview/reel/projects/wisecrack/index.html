Wisecrack

And a Quiplash one, while I'm at it?

When
October 2026
Status
playable
Made with
Node.js (no dependencies), Server-Sent Events, Kokoro TTS, WebAudio

Once the room, the phones, the voice and the music worked for one game, a second game was mostly writing prompts.

1,095prompts
3votes each in the Last Laugh

How it works

  1. 1
    Rounds. Each player gets two prompts per round; each prompt is shared by two players.
  2. 2
    Vote. Answers go head to head. Identical answers are a jinx; a clean sweep earns a bonus.
  3. 3
    Last Laugh. One prompt, or three letters to expand, for everyone, with three votes each.

What I learned

No "safety answer" button. The fun is in writing your own line, even a bad one.
