Who can do this, and what breaks if someone leaves?

When
September 2026
Status
prototype
Made with

The knowledge an organisation needs is usually already inside it, spread over people's heads and tickets. Maieutica draws it out.

4layers in one graph
8views
17slide pitch

How it works

  1. 1
    Model. Four link tables; the people, skill and use-case networks are computed from them.
  2. 2
    Measure. Betweenness and structural holes show who holds things together and where the team is fragile.
  3. 3
    Connect. A Jira or Kanban export feeds the graph, and a proposal can become an epic with stories.

What I learned

Network measures answer management questions surprisingly directly, once the graph is right.
