Spellstorm

Can a survivors-style game work for a five-year-old and a teenager?

When
October 2026
Status
playable
Made with

Play it →

Survivor-style games are simple to pick up and endlessly tunable. I wanted one I could hand to my kids and still enjoy myself, so difficulty is a set of sliders, not a single switch.

4worlds
7 × 8spells × levels
99levels to jump to

How it works

  1. 1
    Worlds. Wizard Academy, Dungeon Delve, Fairy Tale Forest and Candy Kingdom reskin heroes, spells, enemies and music.
  2. 2
    Growth. Seven spells with eight levels each (the eighth evolves), six charms, and enemies that change form every five levels.
  3. 3
    Fairness. From level 3 each enemy resists one element and is weak to another; a coloured ring shows which.
  4. 4
    Together. Local co-op or versus on one screen with gamepads, autosave after each level and save codes to carry progress.

What I learned

Age presets plus sliders work better than easy, medium and hard. Parents and kids disagree about what easy means.
A single HTML file with no build step is the easiest game to share.
