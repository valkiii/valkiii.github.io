Books to my Kindle

Can a chat message put a classic on my Kindle?

When
October 2026
Status
in use
Made with
Python, Telegram Bot API, Gutendex, SMTP

Getting a free classic onto a Kindle takes a browser, a download and an email. A chat message is shorter.

1message: /book_download Title - Author

How it works

  1. 1
    Find. Search Project Gutenberg through Gutendex.
  2. 2
    Fit. Fall back to the image-free EPUB when a book is too large to email.
  3. 3
    Send. Email it to the Kindle address from the Raspberry Pi.
