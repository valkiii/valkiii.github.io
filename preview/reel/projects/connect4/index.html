Four in a row vs five networks

Can I teach a computer to beat me at Connect 4?

When
September 2025
Status
playable
Made with
PyTorch, Dueling Double DQN, CNNs, curriculum learning, JavaScript

Play the ensemble →Code on GitHub ↗

Connect 4 is small enough to learn and big enough that nothing can memorise it: about 4.5 trillion positions. It is the perfect sandbox to see what reinforcement learning really needs.

750ktraining games for the strongest model
35+AI opponents in the tournament
5networks voting on every move

How it works

  1. 1
    Opponents. A random player and a rule-based one set the bar. The rules player beats random 99% of the time.
  2. 2
    Learn. Dueling Double DQNs, first on flat boards, then convolutional networks that see lines like a person does.
  3. 3
    Stabilise. Curriculum learning, reservoir replay and slow target updates against catastrophic forgetting.
  4. 4
    Combine. The five best checkpoints average their Q-values with fixed weights. In this page they run in your browser.

What I learned

An agent only gets as good as its sparring partners.
Ensembles of checkpoints are a cheap way to smooth over a single model's blind spots.
