Market dashboard

What are my shares doing, in euros, from sources I can trust?

When
October 2026
Status
in use
Made with
Python, SQLite, gettex post-trade data, ECB rates, Claude

Free price feeds are in dollars or come with terms I didn't like. Exchanges publish post-trade transparency files; those are enough.

15 minpublic trade files, archived daily
~65companies screened

How it works

  1. 1
    Prices. Public exchange post-trade files archived on the Pi, with ECB rates where an estimate is needed, marked as such.
  2. 2
    Screen. An explicit entry rule: a pullback in an uptrend is interesting, a stock that already exploded is not.
  3. 3
    Ask. Questions typed on the page are answered later by Claude and posted back.

What I learned

A rule I can read beats a score I can't, especially for money.
