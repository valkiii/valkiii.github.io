Market dashboard

What are my shares doing, in euros, from sources I can trust?

Charts for each holding, an earnings calendar and a daily idea screen with written reasons, built only on official, public data.

October 2026in useHome labData science

15 minpublic trade files, archived daily
~65companies screened

The idea

Free price feeds are in dollars or come with terms I didn't like. Exchanges publish post-trade transparency files; those are enough.

How it works

  1. 01Prices

    Public exchange post-trade files archived on the Pi, with ECB rates where an estimate is needed, marked as such.

  2. 02Screen

    An explicit entry rule: a pullback in an uptrend is interesting, a stock that already exploded is not.

  3. 03Ask

    Questions typed on the page are answered later by Claude and posted back.

What I learned

  • A rule I can read beats a score I can't, especially for money.
