Books to my Kindle

Can a chat message put a classic on my Kindle?

A Telegram command that finds a public-domain book on Project Gutenberg and emails the EPUB straight to my Kindle.

October 2026in useHome labTools

1message: /book_download Title - Author

The idea

Getting a free classic onto a Kindle takes a browser, a download and an email. A chat message is shorter.

How it works

  1. 01Find

    Search Project Gutenberg through Gutendex.

  2. 02Fit

    Fall back to the image-free EPUB when a book is too large to email.

  3. 03Send

    Email it to the Kindle address from the Raspberry Pi.

What I learned

  • The best home automations are one sentence long.
