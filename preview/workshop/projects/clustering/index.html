Cluster the reasons

How do you turn a gut-feeling rule into personas a team can use?

Supervised clustering, told as a worked example. A good-enough model explains every customer with SHAP, and the explanations, not the raw data, get clustered.

The idea

Clustering raw customer data gives one overlapping cloud: the biggest and noisiest columns dominate. Asking a model first tells the clustering what matters.

How it works

  1. 01Model

    A gradient-boosted model predicts which product a customer holds. Good enough beats perfect.

  2. 02Explain

    SHAP splits each prediction into one contribution per feature, all in the same unit.

  3. 03Cluster

    UMAP and HDBSCAN on the SHAP matrix: customers group by why they hold a product.

What I learned

  • Every feature in the same unit, and irrelevant ones at zero, is what makes the distance meaningful.
