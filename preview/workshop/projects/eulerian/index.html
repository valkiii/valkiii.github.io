Seeing the invisible

Can a webcam see your pulse?

Eulerian video magnification for clips and live cameras. It amplifies the faint flush of every heartbeat, a baby's breathing, and reads heart rate from a face.

October 2026prototypeSeeingData science

~10 msper frame at 640 px
53 bpmon MIT's face clip (paper: 50–60)
20×phase magnification

The idea

Wu et al. showed in 2012 that ordinary video hides motion and colour changes too small to see. I wanted it live, on my own camera.

How it works

  1. 01Colour

    A per-pixel regression on the face's pulse wave, gated when the head moves, so small movements don't turn into colour blobs.

  2. 02Motion

    Phase-based magnification on a deep Riesz pyramid: more motion, a third of the noise of the linear method.

  3. 03Pulse

    Heart rate from the face with the POS method, and a heat map of where the pulse shows.

What I learned

  • Running averages need bias correction, or the first seconds are ten times too large.
  • Measure motion by tracking an edge, not by counting changed pixels; the second favours the worse method.
