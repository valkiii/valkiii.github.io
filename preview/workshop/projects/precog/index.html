Precog

What if my hands were the mouse?

Minority Report style gesture control for macOS. Swipe between desktops in mid-air, pinch to click, throw windows to the other screen.

The idea

In the film, detectives sort evidence by sweeping their hands through the air. They needed gloves and a wall of glass. A laptop camera and an open hand-tracking model should be enough to try the same thing on an ordinary desk.

How it works

  1. 01See

    MediaPipe turns every frame into 21 points per hand. Nothing after that step looks at pixels.

  2. 02Normalise

    Each hand is redrawn around its wrist and divided by its own size, so near and far hands compare.

  3. 03Recognise

    Poses are matched by shape; movements by dynamic time warping against about six recorded takes, with a threshold set from their spread.

  4. 04Act

    macOS ignores synthetic Ctrl+arrow for desktops, so Precog posts the private dock-swipe events a real trackpad sends.

What I learned

  • Most of the work is not the model. It is making an operating system accept input it was never designed for.
  • Recording gestures by example beats hand-tuned rules, as long as the threshold comes from the examples too.
  • A gesture interface needs an off switch. Ten spread fingers put it on standby.
