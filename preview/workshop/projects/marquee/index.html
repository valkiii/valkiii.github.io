Can a Raspberry Pi be my Plex?

A small Plex-style media server for the Pi. Browse films and shows, resume where you stopped, and play on a laptop, iPhone or Chromecast, at home or away.

October 2026in useHome lab

0.1×realtime: why it never transcodes video
7×realtime audio conversion
0ports open to the internet

The idea

Remote playback in Plex stopped working for me. A server that only does what I need, over my private network, looked smaller than debugging it.

How it works

  1. 01Never transcode

    The Pi decodes HEVC at a tenth of real time, so video is always copied as is and only audio is converted.

  2. 02Cut cleanly

    Segments start on keyframes from a precomputed index, so seeking is instant.

  3. 03Reach

    Tailscale makes it work the same at home and away, with HTTPS and nothing exposed.

What I learned

  • Knowing the hardware's limits up front shapes the whole design.
