Wisecrack

And a Quiplash one, while I'm at it?

Drawbluff's sibling. Two players answer the same prompt, everyone else votes for the funnier one, and the final round gives everyone the same prompt.

October 2026playableGames

1,095prompts
3votes each in the Last Laugh

The idea

Once the room, the phones, the voice and the music worked for one game, a second game was mostly writing prompts.

How it works

  1. 01Rounds

    Each player gets two prompts per round; each prompt is shared by two players.

  2. 02Vote

    Answers go head to head. Identical answers are a jinx; a clean sweep earns a bonus.

  3. 03Last Laugh

    One prompt, or three letters to expand, for everyone, with three votes each.

What I learned

  • No "safety answer" button. The fun is in writing your own line, even a bad one.
