Can you play Fruit Ninja with your eyes?

A Fruit Ninja style browser game. Your fingertips become blades, and an experimental mode lets you slice with your gaze.

The idea

After Precog, the obvious next question was a game. Then a harder one: what if you only had your eyes?

How it works

  1. 01Hands

    MediaPipe's hand landmarker runs in the browser. Only fast swipes cut; slow movement doesn't.

  2. 02Eyes

    A face mesh with irises, calibrated each session on 13 dots and checked on 7 more.

  3. 03Fit

    A small ridge regression maps eye features to the screen; leave-one-dot-out cross-validation picks the model.

  4. 04Cut

    In eye mode a cut is a quick saccade flick or a dwell. Blinks are filtered out.

What I learned

  • Moving from Python to the browser removed a whole class of driver and GPU problems, and gave a smooth 60 fps.
  • Gaze is noisy. Calibration you can verify matters more than a clever model.
