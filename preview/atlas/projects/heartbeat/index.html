My heart beats like a network

What does a heartbeat look like as a network?

Fifty-three minutes of a run, one heart-rate reading at a time, turned into a visibility graph. Peaks become hubs.

The idea

A visibility graph links two points of a time series when they can see each other over the points in between, like rooftops on a street of skyscrapers. I studied networks for years; I wanted to see my own heartbeat as one.

How it works

  1. Record

    One run from my watch: a heart-rate reading every few seconds.

  2. Link

    Two readings connect when nothing in between blocks the line of sight.

  3. Group

    Louvain finds communities; each one is a stretch of the run with its own rhythm.

What I learned

  • The hardest moments of a run become the hubs, because they see furthest.
  • The degree distribution has a heavy tail, slope about −2.5.
