Someone to play with

Could a robot arm sit across the table from me?

The Connect 4 brain, given eyes and an arm. A camera on the wrist reads the board, and an SO-101 arm drops the token.

Read the full story →

3problems: brain, eyes, arm
6joints
1M+games in simulation

The idea

I love board games. My wife doesn't. An app on a screen is not the same as an opponent that looks at the board and moves the pieces itself.

How it works

  1. Brain

    The reinforcement learning agents from Connect 4 decide the move.

  2. Eyes

    A wrist camera finds the board and the tokens and where they sit on the table.

  3. Arm

    Calibration and inverse kinematics turn 'that column' into joint angles.

What I learned

  • Every step from software to the physical world multiplies the ways things fail. Calibration is the real project.
