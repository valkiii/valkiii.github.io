Four in a row vs five networks

Can I teach a computer to beat me at Connect 4?

Deep reinforcement learning agents trained on hundreds of thousands of games. An ensemble of the best five won a tournament against 35 other AIs, and you can play it here.

The idea

Connect 4 is small enough to learn and big enough that nothing can memorise it: about 4.5 trillion positions. It is the perfect sandbox to see what reinforcement learning really needs.

How it works

  1. Opponents

    A random player and a rule-based one set the bar. The rules player beats random 99% of the time.

  2. Learn

    Dueling Double DQNs, first on flat boards, then convolutional networks that see lines like a person does.

  3. Stabilise

    Curriculum learning, reservoir replay and slow target updates against catastrophic forgetting.

  4. Combine

    The five best checkpoints average their Q-values with fixed weights. In this page they run in your browser.

What I learned

  • An agent only gets as good as its sparring partners.
  • Ensembles of checkpoints are a cheap way to smooth over a single model's blind spots.
