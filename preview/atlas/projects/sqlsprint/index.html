SQL Sprint

Can you prepare for a live SQL interview in a week?

A 7-day crash course that runs a real SQLite engine in the browser, with graded exercises, interview traps and two timed mock interviews.

49graded exercises
2timed mock interviews
32concept questions

The idea

I had a live SQL coding interview coming. The best preparation is writing queries against data and being told exactly what's wrong.

How it works

  1. Learn

    Six days from foundations to window functions and product metrics: retention, funnels, A/B readouts, churn.

  2. Check

    'Check answer' compares your output with the solution and says what's off: row count, order, rounding, integer division.

  3. Rehearse

    Day 7 is two 45-minute mock interviews with the clarifying questions to ask.

What I learned

  • Feedback on why an answer is wrong teaches more than the right answer.
  • It now sells as an offline course.
