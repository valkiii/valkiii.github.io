Local Voice Transcriber

Can dictation be private and pretty?

A minimal desktop app that records your voice and transcribes it offline with Whisper. Click the glowing ball, speak, copy the text.

Code on GitHub ↗

The idea

Speech to text shouldn't need an account. Whisper runs fine on a laptop.

How it works

  1. Record

    Pick a microphone, click the ball; it pulses as you speak.

  2. Transcribe

    Whisper runs locally; nothing leaves the machine.

  3. Keep

    Each transcript is saved with a timestamp for its name.
