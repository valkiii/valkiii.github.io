Drawbluff

Can I host a Drawful-style party game from a laptop?

A party game for 3 to 10 players. Everyone draws a secret prompt on their phone, invents fake titles for the others' drawings, then tries to spot the real one.

3–10players
1,251prompts
0npm packages needed to play

The idea

Party games are best on a TV with everyone's phone as the controller. I wanted one that runs from my own laptop, without accounts, app stores or a subscription.

How it works

  1. Join

    The TV shows a QR code. Phones scan it and join with a four-letter room code.

  2. Draw

    Everyone draws themselves first, then a secret prompt. Bots fill empty seats.

  3. Bluff

    For each drawing, players write a title others might mistake for the real one, then vote.

  4. Host

    A local Kokoro voice presents the game, over a soundtrack generated in WebAudio.

What I learned

  • Server-Sent Events plus plain POST requests are enough for a real-time party game.
  • Wording matters. Players found "write a lie" off-putting; "write a title" felt like a game.
