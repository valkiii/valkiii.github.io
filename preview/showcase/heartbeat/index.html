Peaks become hubs

A reading sees far when it stands taller than its neighbours. So the hardest moments of the run turn into the graph's hubs: The flat, steady stretches only see the readings next to them, and they knit into tight local clusters.

That is why the network breaks so cleanly into communities: each one is a stretch of the run with its own rhythm. Switch the colour to Communities and they line up along the strip almost in order of time.

The degree distribution has a heavy tail. Most readings see a handful of others, a few see more than fifty. On log-log axes the tail falls roughly on a line with slope , the kind of scale-free signature visibility graphs pick up from fractal-like series.

Data: one Garmin TCX export, heart rate only (GPS removed). Visibility follows Lacasa et al., PNAS 2008, using real elapsed seconds as the x axis (the watch samples every ~4.6 s, unevenly). Communities: Louvain, seed 7. Layouts: seeded force layout with a pull towards each community, and a parametric heart where time runs clockwise from the tip.
