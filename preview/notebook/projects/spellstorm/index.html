Spellstorm

Can a survivors-style game work for a five-year-old and a teenager?

A wizard survival game in the browser. Spells cast themselves while you dodge hordes, level up and face a boss at the end of every level.

play it →

games

the idea

Survivor-style games are simple to pick up and endlessly tunable. I wanted one I could hand to my kids and still enjoy myself, so difficulty is a set of sliders, not a single switch.

  • 4worlds
  • 7 × 8spells × levels
  • 99levels to jump to

how it works

  1. Worlds

    Wizard Academy, Dungeon Delve, Fairy Tale Forest and Candy Kingdom reskin heroes, spells, enemies and music.

  2. Growth

    Seven spells with eight levels each (the eighth evolves), six charms, and enemies that change form every five levels.

  3. Fairness

    From level 3 each enemy resists one element and is weak to another; a coloured ring shows which.

  4. Together

    Local co-op or versus on one screen with gamepads, autosave after each level and save codes to carry progress.

notes to self

  • Age presets plus sliders work better than easy, medium and hard. Parents and kids disagree about what easy means.
  • A single HTML file with no build step is the easiest game to share.
