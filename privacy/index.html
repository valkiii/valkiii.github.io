Privacy

What this site
keeps.

When you open a page, your browser tells my own small server, running at home, which page you opened, its title, the page you came from, your screen width and your browser's language. The server adds the time, your IP address and your browser's user agent.

I use this only to see how many people visit, which pages they read and where they come from. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and is not shared with anyone or used for advertising. Visits are deleted after 180 days.

Comments are handled by giscus and stored as GitHub Discussions, so commenting needs a GitHub account and follows GitHub's privacy statement. The comment box only loads when you scroll to it.

The site itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. To ask what is stored about you, or to have it removed, write to me on LinkedIn.
